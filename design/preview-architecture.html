How the site fits together

Events move to the centre. Every page leads to the open event, the event page takes the registration, and one admin screen is where the club checks payments.

What visitors see

  • Home
    Hero, the open event, about, awards, gallery, contact in the footer
  • Events
    Open, upcoming and past, in that order
  • Event page
    Facts, how it works, and the registration form
  • About, Awards, Gallery
    The club's story, its shelf, its photos
  • Contact
    Address, phone, email, map

English and Malayalam on every page.

Registering

  1. 1
    Sees the open event
    On Home, in Events, or from a shared link
  2. 2
    Fills in the form
    Name, phone, category, team
  3. 3
    Pays by UPI
    In their own UPI app, then adds the reference and a screenshot
  4. 4
    Submits
    Saved as "to check"; the page says what happens next
  5. 5
    Club verifies
    A volunteer checks the payment and calls to confirm

No money moves through the site.

What the club runs

  • Sign in
    Club officers only
  • Registrations
    Check payments, mark verified, export the list
  • Events
    Create, open and close registration
  • Awards
    Add to the shelf
  • Gallery
    Upload and caption photos

Until this is built, registrations are read in the Firebase console.

Where it is kept

  • events
    Title, date, venue, status: upcoming, open, closed
  • registrations
    Who, which event, UPI reference, payment status
  • awards and gallery
    Records in both languages, with images
  • File storage
    Payment screenshots and photos
  • Rules
    Anyone may add a registration; only officers may read them

Firebase: Firestore, Storage, Auth, Hosting.
